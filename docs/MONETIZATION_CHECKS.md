# Publishing readiness checks (reviewed 2026-11-09)

Shorts Factory produces authorized short videos quickly; it **never certifies copyright clearance, account monetization or earnings**. Video format rules are only one aspect of eligibility. The self-hosted service has no connection to the user’s Facebook, Instagram, YouTube or TikTok accounts or to copyrighted-content fingerprint databases.

## Check modes

- **Automatic technical checks** on actual exported files: H.264 video, 1080×1920 portrait dimensions, expected audio codec/presence, file size and expected duration.
- **Automated review flags**: chosen destination, clip length, language/transcript cues and potential profanity, absent captions, weak speech timing, music-video title cues, source-rights self-declarations, originality self-declarations and covered previous logos.
- **Needs human verification**: copyright/neighboring rights, master recording and composition licenses, whether a work is substantially transformed, rights to remove earlier branding, advertiser suitability, account standing, platform-program enrollment, claims and commercial restrictions.
- **Not possible to guarantee**: approval, ad inventory, qualified views, payouts, copyright matches, reach or monetization of each clip.

## Facebook Reels and content monetization

Meta's March 2026 original-content guidance says that adding borders, captions or speed changes to another creator's video is insufficient, and merely watching or reacting with facial expressions is also inadequate. Substantial analysis, new information or a meaningful storyline can be valuable but still requires individual review. Facebook Content Monetization is invite-only, with an interest form in the mobile Professional Dashboard; not every posted Reel is paid.

## Instagram Reels

Instagram does not have one universal payout-for-views setting. Gifts, subscriptions, invitations and other products have different account, regional and policy requirements. Check the account's Professional Dashboard and specific feature eligibility. Rights/originality policies still apply. This studio does not infer followers, country eligibility, age or professional-account status from a video.

Sources:

## YouTube Shorts

YouTube checks YPP/Shorts Monetization Module enrollment, eligible views, advertiser-friendly content, channel originality and copyright. The reused-content policy is **separate** from copyright permission and applies even if footage was licensed when its reuse adds little new value. A clip with a new logo and subtitles is **not automatically original**. Shorts up to three minutes may qualify under YouTube's current video format rules; music claims can change the outcome.

Sources:
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12504220
- https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2490020

## TikTok Creator Rewards

The Creator Rewards Program requires original, high-quality videos **longer than 60 seconds**, and eligibility also depends on the account and program rules. Selecting TikTok changes Shorts Factory's target to 90 seconds automatically, but it cannot create more original footage when the source is too short and will flag finished clips of 60 seconds or less.

Source:
- https://support.tiktok.com/en/business-and-creator/creator-rewards-program/how-is-the-creator-rewards-program-different-from-the-tiktok-creator-fund

## Branding and authorized changes

- The built-in Bajo Mundo TV artwork comes from the owner's separate TV site repository. Transparency processing preserves the artwork and removes a connected white background, then renders it on new Shorts.
- A saved channel profile chooses a different logo without modifying past exports.
- Optional blurring of a previous **corner** logo is available only when footage rights are selected as owned or licensed. It covers a small area in the **final output**; it cannot identify arbitrary marks, remove audio tags, prove permission, or make reused third-party footage suitable for monetization.
- Rights and originality answers are self-reported. Do not remove attribution or other creators' marks from unlicensed footage and present it as original.

**Workflow:** Select channel and destination → upload authorized source → choose framing/captioning → start batch → inspect each actual export and its risk report → confirm rights and platform monetization status before publishing.

Content and programs may change. Re-verify platform policies before relying on this document.
